The house

Built for slow mornings and leisurely evenings

Built for seamless entertaining, the house spans three floors, boasting five bedrooms, seven beds, five bathrooms (three en-suite) and two kitchens. A perfect pool and hot tub with extensive lounge seating rounds out the perfect vacation experience. Add in a short 8 minute walk down to the beach and boardwalk to get the whole Praia da Luz experience.

Bedrooms

Five bedrooms, four with ocean views and private balconies, three with en-suite baths. With a total of seven beds (1 king, 2 queens, 2 doubles, 2 singles), the house can sleep twelve adults comfortably.

Outdoor space

Jump in the sun-soaked pool and hot tub, rinse off in the outdoor shower, then lounge in one of the multiple outdoor seating areas.

Kitchen & dining

Two large indoor kitchens with dining and living areas on the first floor and lower level. The lower level walks out to the pool area with its own outdoor kitchen and dining setup.

Getting there

An easy 50-minute drive from Faro Airport, or 2.5 hours from Lisbon, almost entirely via Portugal's modern high-speed roadways.

Location

Praia da Luz, Algarve
